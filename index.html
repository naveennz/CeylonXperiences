Our Mission

Connecting the world's travelers with Sri Lanka's most authentic living traditions — one intimate experience at a time.

CeylonXperiences was founded in Colombo to solve a persistent discrepancy in local tourism: while travelers search for deep cultural connection, true generation-line artisans rarely benefit from the traditional high-margin hotel tour agency model. By digitising reservations and organising small-group itineraries, we make traditional arts micro-sustainable while ensuring travellers receive authentic, un-staged interactions with real masters.

Terms & Operating Guidelines

1. Booking and Financial Distribution

All values are locked in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). Out of every payment transaction, exactly 70% is issued immediately via institutional banking ledger directly to the primary hosting artisan within 48 hours of successful execution.

2. Cancellation Protocols

Travelers retain full refund rights up to 48 working hours preceding the booked slot. Inside the 48-hour window, cancellations prompt a 50% reservation hold-fee designed to secure host raw material expenditure costs.

3. Guest Conduct

All guests are expected to treat artisan studios, tools, and hosts with respect. CeylonXperiences reserves the right to remove guests who cause damage or distress to hosting artisans from future bookings without refund.

4. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a dispute, our local Experience Quality team mediates within 2 business days. Sri Lankan consumer protection law applies to all transactions.
