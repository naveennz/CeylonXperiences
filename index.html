Our Mission

CeylonXperiences was founded in Colombo to solve a persistent discrepancy in local tourism: while travelers search for deep cultural connection, true generation-line artisans rarely benefit from traditional high-margin hotel tour booking agencies.

By digitizing reservations and organizing small-group itineraries, we make traditional arts micro-sustainable while ensuring travelers receive authentic, un-staged interactions with real masters.

Terms & Operating Guidelines

1. Booking and Financial Distribution

All values are locked in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). Out of every payment transaction, exactly 70% is issued immediately via institutional banking ledger directly to the primary hosting artisan within 48 hours of successful execution.

2. Cancellation Protocols

Travelers retain full refund rights up to 48 working hours preceding zero hour of the booked slot. Inside the 48-hour window, cancellations prompt a 50% reservation hold-fee designed to secure host raw material expenditure structures.
